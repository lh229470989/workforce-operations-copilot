# AcmeWorks Flexible Work and Overtime Policy

## Collaboration hours

Teams choose their work location, but employees should normally be reachable
from 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone on working days. A department may
publish a different collaboration window when its operating needs require it.

## Overtime

Overtime must be approved in writing by the employee's manager before the
extra work begins, except during a documented service incident. Incident
overtime must be reported to the manager on the next working day.

## Recording overtime

Approved overtime is recorded against the project that benefited from the
work. The time entry description should include the approval reference or the
fictional incident identifier. Recording overtime does not itself constitute
manager approval.
