---

# Requesting planned leave

Employees should submit planned leave at least five business days before the first day away. The employee's manager approves the request; People Operations is consulted only for exceptions or extended leave.

# Recording time during leave

Approved leave is not recorded against a customer or internal delivery project. Employees should leave project time blank for the approved leave hours and use the separate leave system referenced by AcmeWorks policy.

# Emergencies

Unexpected absence should be reported to the manager as soon as practical. A late notice caused by an emergency is not automatically treated as a policy violation.
