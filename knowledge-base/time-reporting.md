# AcmeWorks Time Reporting Policy

## Weekly submission deadline

Employees submit the previous week's time entries by 12:00 noon local time on
Monday. If Monday is an AcmeWorks holiday, the deadline moves to 12:00 noon on
the next working day.

## Entry quality

Each entry must identify one authorized project, the work date, the number of
hours, and a short description of the outcome. An employee may record no more
than 24 hours for one calendar day. Draft entries do not count as submitted.

## Review and corrections

Managers review submitted entries for their direct reports by Wednesday
17:00 local time. Employees cannot approve their own entries. A manager who
needs a correction rejects the entry with a reason; the employee then edits
and resubmits it.

## Late submissions

An employee who expects to miss the deadline should notify their manager
before the deadline. The manager may record a fictional demo exception, but
the employee must still submit complete entries.
