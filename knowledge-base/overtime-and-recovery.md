title: AcmeWorks Overtime and Recovery Policy
---

# Prior approval

Overtime must be approved by the employee's manager before the work begins, except during a documented service incident. Project urgency alone does not waive the approval requirement.

# Time reporting

All hours actually worked must still be reported accurately against the relevant project. Employees must not reduce or move hours merely to fit a daily target.

# Recovery time

After exceptional evening or weekend work, the manager should agree on reasonable recovery time within the following two weeks. Recovery time does not erase the original time record.
