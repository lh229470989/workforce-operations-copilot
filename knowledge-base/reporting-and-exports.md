title: AcmeWorks Reporting and Export Policy
---

# Role scope

Employees may export only their own visible time records. Managers may export records for themselves and their direct reports. Administrators may export all fictional AcmeWorks records.

# Data minimization

Exports should use the narrowest practical date and project filters. Export files are intended for the local demonstration and must not be combined with real employee or customer data.

# Retention

Downloaded demonstration reports should be deleted when they are no longer needed. The demo does not provide centralized retention or remote audit shipping.
